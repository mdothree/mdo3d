Example reading

What a $5 Premium Single Card Reading looks like. This is an example, generated with Claude (Opus 5.5) on 2026-10-14 using the same prompt this app uses, for the sample input shown. Your reading is written for your own question.

Sample question: “I keep saying yes to extra work. How do I protect my energy without letting my team down?” Card drawn: Boundaries.

Opening

You asked how to protect your energy without letting people down, and the card that answered is Boundaries. That is a kind answer: it suggests the problem is not how much you care, but where your care has no edges.

Interpretation

Boundaries is the card of the clear line. It speaks of a fence with a gate in it, not a wall. In your situation it points to the moment just before you say yes: the half-second where you already know the answer should be “not this week” and say yes anyway. The card does not ask you to stop helping. It asks you to decide in advance what help costs, so that your yes means something again.

Insights

  • Saying yes to everything makes your yes worth less to the people who rely on it.
  • Your team is more likely to be let down by your exhaustion than by a clear “not today”.
  • A boundary stated early sounds like planning; stated late, it sounds like a complaint.

Action steps

  • Before your next yes, pause and name what you will drop to make room for it.
  • Choose one recurring request this week and hand it back with a suggested owner.
  • Block one protected hour on your calendar and treat it like a meeting.
  • Tell one colleague what you are changing, so the boundary is shared, not secret.

Affirmation

I give fully where I choose to give, and my limits make my help trustworthy.
