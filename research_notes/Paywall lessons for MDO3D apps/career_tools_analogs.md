# Career-Tool Analogs: Monetization, Gating, Trust, Traffic (for rigor.design)

Research date: 2026-10-06. Many vendor pricing pages block automated fetches (Teal 403, Jobscan redirects into app, Resume Worded 404), so several price points come from third-party reviews/aggregators and are flagged as such. Primary-source fetches succeeded for Kickresume, Rezi, Final Round AI.

## 1. Free vs paid boundaries, prices, billing periods, paywall placement

### Takeaway
The category clusters at $19–$50/month list price, with heavy discounts for quarterly (~$18–$30/mo effective) and, uniquely for job seekers, weekly plans (Teal) and lifetime/one-time options (Rezi $149, Big Interview $299). The dominant free-tier pattern is "score/analysis free, AI fix + export gated", with metered free quotas (e.g., Jobscan ~5 scans/mo, Rezi 3 PDF downloads). rigor.design's $9.99/mo sits well below every comparable except Yoodli ($8–$20) and annualized Kickresume ($8/mo yearly).

### Cited Findings
**Resume / ATS scoring**
- Jobscan: free plan ~5 resume scans/month with basic match rate + keyword suggestions; paid $49.95/month or $89.95/quarter (~$29.98/mo); paid unlocks unlimited scans, "One-Click Optimize" AI rewriting, cover letter generator, LinkedIn optimizer, job tracker. No pro-rated refunds if you land a job early. (Third-party; Jobscan's own pricing page redirects into the logged-in app — itself a signal that pricing is shown post-signup.) — [Interview Guys review 2026](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/?p=10428); [jobshinobi](https://jobshinobi.com/compare/is-jobscan-resume-scanner-worth-it)
- Resume Worded: free tier gives a partial score/partial report; Pro ~$19/month, also quarterly and annual ($229/yr); Pro unlocks full "Score My Resume" report, line-by-line analysis, LinkedIn Review, AI writer. Classic "score shown, details gated." (Third-party) — [Resume Worded review](https://www.loopcv.pro/directory/resume-worded/); [hirequotient](https://www.hirequotient.com/blog/resume-worded-review)
- Rezi (primary): Free = 1 resume, 1 AI interview, 3 PDF downloads, "limited" Rezi Score/keyword targeting; Pro $29/month (unlimited + 1 expert review/mo); Lifetime $149 one-time; Enterprise $99/mo per 200 users (universities/agencies); 30-day 100% money-back guarantee. — [Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing)
- Teal: free forever with unlimited resumes and job tracking but capped AI credits (reported: 10 bullet, 2 summary, 2 cover letter generations); Teal+ unlimited AI. Price reports conflict: "$9/week, $29/month, $79/quarter" vs "$13 every 7 days, $29 every 30 days, $79 every 90 days". Note 30-day/90-day cycles, not calendar months. (Third-party; Teal pricing page returned 403) — [dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-ai-resume-builders); [frontdeskreview June 2026](https://frontdeskreview.com/software/resume-builders/teal/); [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/teal-hq/pricing)

**Salary negotiation**
- Levels.fyi negotiation services: Standard $1,250 ("$10k guaranteed increase"), Premium $2,450 ($15k), Leadership $5,000 ($40k); salary data itself is free (SEO magnet). — [Levels.fyi services](https://www.levels.fyi/services?from=tech); [igotanoffer review](https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/levelsfyi-alternatives-review)

### Inferences
- Paywall placements observed: (a) metered free quota then wall (Jobscan, Rezi, Yoodli, Teal AI credits); (b) score visible, detail/fix gated (Resume Worded, Jobscan's One-Click Optimize, LinkedIn job-fit); (c) build free, export/download gated or watermarked (Zety, Enhancv); (d) setup free, core live use gated with no trial (Final Round AI). (c) generates the most complaints (see section 3); (b) is the cleanest fit for rigor.design's tools.
- Short horizon: vendors address it with weekly (Teal), 30/90-day cycles, quarterly discounts, lifetime (Rezi, Big Interview), and money-back guarantees. A $9.99/mo monthly-only plan is already cheaper than most competitors' weekly price; a quarterly ($24.99) or one-time "job-search pass" could capture users who distrust subscriptions.
- 3 free uses/month is in line with Rezi (3 downloads) and below Jobscan (~5 scans) and Yoodli (5/week). Per-tool vs global quota matters: if each subdomain gives 3 uses, a user gets 21/month free, which may undercut Pro.
- Guaranteed-outcome pricing (Levels.fyi) and human-review add-ons (Rezi expert review, Kickresume proofreading) are higher-margin upsells that a pure-AI tool lacks.

### Gaps
- Primary pricing pages for Teal, Jobscan, Resume Worded, Enhancv, Zety, Novorésumé, Careerflow, Huntr not fetchable; figures above are third-party and partly conflicting (Teal weekly $9 vs $13; Enhancv two price sets; Jobscan free scan count has varied historically).
- Careerflow pricing not found. Novorésumé pricing not retrieved.
- Final Round AI's exact monthly/quarterly/annual prices not on fetched page.

## 2. Evidence of what converts; public revenue/user numbers

### Takeaway
There is no published A/B data from these vendors on "show score, gate fix"; the evidence is revealed preference (nearly every successful player gives a free score/scan and gates rewriting/export) plus revenue figures showing small, bootstrapped tools can reach low-millions ARR. Numbers below are mostly self-reported or Latka-type estimates.

### Cited Findings
- Rezi: bootstrapped ($0 funding), ~$2.4M revenue and 150K customers in 2023; ~$215K MRR (Latka/Starter Story — founder self-reported). Started as a ~$9.69 Word doc template sold via Reddit. — [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder); [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/rezi)
- Kickresume: bootstrapped, ~$1.9M revenue 2024 (Latka estimate). — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/kickresume)
- Teal: $7.5M Series A Jan 2025, total ~$19M raised; funds for AI Interview Coach and Salary Negotiation tools; 2M+ members (other sources say ~4M); revenue reports conflict ($16M 2023 vs $5.5M) — treat as unverified estimates. — [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/teal-raises-7-5m-for-ai-careers-platform); [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/tealhq.com)
- Final Round AI: $6.88M seed (Jan 2025) led by Uncork Capital; no reliable ARR found. — [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/final-round-ai/funding-rounds/seed-2025-01)
- Simplify Copilot extension: 1M+ Chrome installs, 4.9 rating (March 2026). — [stork.ai comparison](https://www.stork.ai/compare/simplify-copilot-vs-teal); [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/simplify-copilot-autofill/pbanhockgagggenencehbnadejlgchfc)
- Kickresume's most popular plan is yearly at $8/mo — evidence users accept long commitments when the per-month price is low. — [Kickresume pricing](https://www.kickresume.com/en/pricing/)

### Inferences
- Rezi (~$2.4M at ~$29/mo list + $149 lifetime) suggests a single focused tool can support a small team; for rigor.design at $9.99, ~2,000 paying subs ≈ $240K ARR — reaching Rezi-scale needs ~20K subs, so lower price must be compensated by volume or higher tiers.
- The near-universal "free score/scan" is the acquisition hook; the paid unit is the AI rewrite, unlimited usage, or export.

### Gaps
- No public conversion rates (free-to-paid %) for any of these tools. No vendor A/B results on blurred vs unblurred reports.
- Jobscan, Resume Worded, Enhancv, Final Round AI revenue not found from reliable sources.

## 3. Trust/privacy of resumes and controversies

### Takeaway
Leaders disclose third-party LLM processing (OpenAI named) in privacy policies; the stronger ones state contractual no-training terms. The big trust failures are billing, not privacy: Zety-style low-price trials converting to ~$24/4-week renewals after users build a resume, and Final Round AI's "stealth" live-interview cheating positioning plus an alleged competitor smear.

### Cited Findings
- Teal's privacy policy says it collects resume content, cover letters, job descriptions, and uses third-party AI providers such as OpenAI. — [Teal privacy (ToS tracker copy)](https://tostracker.app/document/tealhqcom-privacy)
- Resume Worded names AI providers incl. OpenAI and says they are contractually restricted from training on API data beyond brief abuse-monitoring retention (via third-party summary). — [venture-lab](https://venture-lab.org/2026/resume-scanner-data-retention-questions/amp/)
- Jobscan: users can archive scan history and request profile/scan-data deletion via support (not self-serve). — [venture-lab](https://venture-lab.org/2026/resume-scanner-data-retention-questions/amp/)
- Yoodli sells "added privacy controls" as part of its $20 Advanced tier — privacy as a paid feature. — [outdoo review](https://www.outdoo.ai/blog/yoodli-review-and-pricing)
- Zety complaints: charges after "one-time" payment, recurring €23.95 charges, cancellation ignored; build-then-download-paywall-then-hard-to-cancel pattern; German consumer site called it a subscription trap ("Abofalle"). — [Sikayetvar complaints](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/zety-us/zetycom-charges-unauthorized-monthly-subscription-fees-after-one-time-resume-creation); [Mimikama](https://www.mimikama.org/en/lebenslauf-editor-zety-fuehrt-in-die-abofalle/); [terms.law](https://terms.law/ToS-Watchdog/resume-builders/zety/)
- No specific FTC action against Zety was found in my searches.
- BBB: ~40,000 complaints tied to subscriptions/free trials/recurring charges over 24 months; easy signup/hard cancel is the core pattern. — [KOMO/BBB](https://komonews.com/news/consumer/bbb-digital-subscription-warning-subscription-and-auto-pay-complaints-rise-as-consumers-struggle-to-cancel-what-to-do-washington-state)
- Final Round AI: markets "Stealth Mode" and live Interview CoPilot (real-time help during actual interviews). — [Final Round AI pricing](https://www.finalroundai.com/pricing). A 2026 press-release-style piece alleges FinalRound-affiliated creators amplified an unfounded InterviewCoder "data leak" scare; Trustpilot themes include unexpected charges and hidden cancellation terms. Source is a syndicated PR (ABNewswire) likely from a competitor — treat as low-reliability. — [Barchart/ABNewswire](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/518947/interview-coder-vs-finalround-ai-undetectable-or-undermined)
- Regulation: FTC Click-to-Cancel rule was vacated by the 8th Circuit on July 8, 2025 (procedural grounds) before its July 14 effective date. — [Fenwick](https://www.fenwick.com/insights/publications/eighth-circuit-vacates-ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule). ROSCA and state laws still apply. California AB 2863 (contracts on/after July 1, 2025): express affirmative consent to renewal terms with proof kept ≥3 years, annual reminders, notice of price changes, online "click to cancel", and covers free-to-paid conversions. — [Kilpatrick Townsend](https://ktslaw.com/insights/alert/2024/10/california-latest-automatic-renewal-law-amendments-take-effect-in-july-2025)

### Inferences
- For rigor.design: plain monthly $9.99 with no trial-to-paid conversion avoids the riskiest pattern; still need a CA-compliant consent checkbox, online one-click cancel, renewal reminder emails, and stored consent proof.
- Privacy differentiator: state up front (at upload) which LLM vendor processes the resume, that API data is not used for training, retention period, and offer self-serve delete — Jobscan's support-ticket deletion is a gap to beat. Optionally offer PII redaction (strip name/email/phone before the LLM call).
- Avoid "cheating" positioning (Final Round AI) — reputational risk with employers and press.

### Gaps
- Could not read full primary privacy policies for Jobscan, Teal, Rezi, Kickresume; retention periods not confirmed.
- No verified press coverage (TechCrunch etc.) of a Final Round AI marketing scandal; only low-reliability PR.

## 4. Traffic acquisition; suite of 7 subdomains vs one product

### Takeaway
Traffic in this category is won by programmatic SEO content (resume examples, templates, salary pages) on a single strong domain, plus Chrome extensions that live inside job boards. Incumbents are converging into suites (Teal adding interview + negotiation; Jobscan bundling cover letter + LinkedIn + tracker; Rezi adding AI interview). Seven separate subdomains fragment SEO authority and pricing comprehension.

### Cited Findings
- Zety.com ~1.6M organic traffic, DR 76 (Ahrefs estimate); competitors Resume Genius, Resume.io, Novorésumé, ResumeBuilder all compete on example/template content. — [Ahrefs zety.com](https://ahrefs.com/websites/zety.com/competitors)
- Levels.fyi uses free salary data to drive traffic to $1,250–$5,000 negotiation services. — [Levels.fyi services](https://www.levels.fyi/services?from=tech)
- Kickresume's free tier advertises "1,500+ resume examples" — SEO content as part of the product. — [Kickresume pricing](https://www.kickresume.com/en/pricing/)
- Simplify grew via a free autofill extension (1M+ installs); Teal's extension saves jobs from LinkedIn/Indeed/Glassdoor. — [stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/compare/simplify-copilot-vs-teal)
- Bundling: Teal's Series A explicitly funds AI Interview Coach and Salary Negotiation; Jobscan's paid tier bundles cover letter, LinkedIn optimizer, tracker; Rezi bundles AI interview into its resume plan. — [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/teal-raises-7-5m-for-ai-careers-platform); [Interview Guys](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/?p=10428); [Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing)
- Google's John Mueller: subdomains and subdirectories are "in general" treated the same, but he prefers keeping things together and using subdomains only for things that are "slightly different"; practitioners note subdomains are verified/treated as separate sites and authority does not reliably flow. — [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-treats-subdomains-subdirectories-john-mueller-says); [Bruce Clay](https://www.bruceclay.com/blog/subdomains-vs-subdirectories-seo/)

### Inferences
- A single "Pro covers all 7 tools" plan is aligned with the market (suites); but 7 subdomains with separate landing pages dilutes link equity and makes a $9.99 bundle harder to explain. Recommend subdirectories (rigor.design/resume, /cover-letter, ...) or at least one shared hub, shared auth/session, cross-tool upsells after each result ("your resume scored 72 — now generate a tailored cover letter").
- Single-purpose landing pages do help paid/SEO intent matching; that can be achieved with per-tool subfolders without splitting domains.
- Cheapest durable channels: free score tools as SEO landing pages, example/template pages, a Chrome extension for LinkedIn/job boards, university/career-center enterprise licenses (Rezi Enterprise model).

### Gaps
- No case study directly comparing multi-subdomain suites vs single domain in career tools.
- No reliable traffic numbers for Jobscan/Teal/Final Round AI retrieved.

## 5. Sign-in before first use: friction evidence

### Takeaway
Evidence (mostly practitioner/case-study, not rigorous) favors letting users reach a first result before requiring an account, then capturing sign-in within the first session (e.g., to save, see full details, or export). Several category leaders still require an account for their core scan (Jobscan's pricing even lives inside the app), but the free score is usually reachable quickly.

### Cited Findings
- Case study: removing a signup wall moved signup conversion from ~3% to ~30% for an AI doc tool (single founder anecdote). — [DEV Community DocPilot](https://dev.to/clarrydaro/how-i-increased-docpilots-conversion-rate-by-removing-the-signup-wall-34l1)
- Claim that 64% of users drop off during a typical SaaS signup flow before seeing the product; recommendation to place signup deeper in the first session because session-2 return without an account drops sharply. (Agency blog; methodology unclear) — [925studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/sign-up-flow-examples-that-convert)
- Attribution downside: anonymous-first users who return later show up as "cold direct signups." — [Product Hunt discussion](https://www.producthunt.com/p/general/if-people-can-use-your-product-before-signing-up-how-do-you-attribute-the-signup)
- AuthoredUp offers a 14-day trial with no card; Taplio 7-day full trial — trial without card is common for LinkedIn tools. — [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/pricing); [Taplio](https://taplio.com/blog/taplio-pricing)
- Jobscan's /pricing redirects to app.jobscan.co/plan (observed during research), i.e., plan details sit behind the app. — [app.jobscan.co/plan](https://app.jobscan.co/plan)

### Inferences
- For rigor.design: consider 1 anonymous use (rate-limited by IP/device fingerprint, no storage of resume), showing the score, then require Google one-tap sign-in to see the full fix, save, or use the remaining free uses. This keeps abuse control (LLM cost) while removing the first-use wall. Resume PII handling for anonymous users should default to no retention.
- If sign-in stays mandatory, use one-tap OAuth and explain why ("so we can delete your resume on request / keep your 3 free runs").

### Gaps
- No controlled study specific to career tools on signup-first vs try-first. The 3%→30% and 64% figures are anecdotal/unsourced and should not be presented as benchmarks.
