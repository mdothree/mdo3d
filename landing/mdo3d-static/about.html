About

Building AI platforms, one domain at a time.

MDO3D is an independent studio building a family of AI-powered platforms across four areas: spiritual guidance, professional development, everyday productivity, and commerce. Each platform is a focused product designed to do one thing well.

What we're working on

Our guidance platforms bring thoughtful, AI-assisted interpretation to practices like tarot, dream analysis, oracle cards, and numerology. Our professional tools help people sharpen resumes and prepare for interviews. Our utility suite at mdothree.com gives developers and makers fast, privacy-respecting tools for color, hashing, QR codes, PDFs, and more.

How we build

We ship small, keep tools fast, and lean toward doing things in the browser so your data stays with you wherever possible. Many of our core tools are free to use, with optional paid tiers for people who want more.

We're always happy to hear from people using our products. Reach us at hello@mdo3d.com.
