## Cross-cutting themes for social

- **"Many dimensions of intelligence"** — one brand, many AI tools. The
  ecosystem angle is the story: not a single app, but a growing family.
- **AI-native everywhere.** Every tool leads with an AI capability — good hook
  material ("AI that reads your dream symbols," "AI that scores your leads").
- **Two audiences under one roof** — spiritual/self-reflection *and*
  professional/productivity. Content can lean into either voice, but the violet
  brand keeps it coherent.
- **Prioritize 🟢 Live tools** for conversion posts; use 🟡 Soon for teasers and
  waitlist/launch-countdown content. Avoid 🔵 Dev tools in public posts.
