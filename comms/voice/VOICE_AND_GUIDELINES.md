# MDO3D Voice and Post Guidelines

**Status:** Scaffold. The mechanical rules below are live and enforced by
`review/voice_qc.py`. The brand register and per-tool claims are TODO, to be filled when
MDO3D social content and its brand voice are defined.

**Scope:** Social and outbound copy for the MDO3D portfolio.

---

## 1. Register (defined 2026-08-17)

**The studio voice (the LinkedIn `/company/mdo3` page speaks in this one).** MDO3D posts as
**a maker running a small AI studio**: plainspoken, specific, a little wry, technically
honest. It shows the building. It never adopts a mystical or hype register, even when the
subject is a divination tool. The engineer's voice, not the oracle's. Reader: someone who
builds software, hires, or is job hunting, and is curious what a one-person AI studio looks
like week to week. Open with a plain observation; close open and non-promotional. See
[`../../social/MDO3D_SOCIAL_STRATEGY.md`](../../social/MDO3D_SOCIAL_STRATEGY.md).

**Per-brand register** (for on-product copy and future consumer channels; the studio page
uses the studio voice above regardless of which product it is discussing):

| Brand | Reader & intent | Tone | Opening move | Close |
|-------|-----------------|------|--------------|-------|
| **Rigor** (career: rigor.design) | A job seeker at the moment before they hit send | Practical, credible, encouraging without flattery | Name the concrete stakes ("software reads your resume before a human does") | A specific next action |
| **mdothree** (utilities) | A developer or knowledge worker mid-task | Terse, useful, no fluff | State the job the tool does | The tool, no pitch |
| **Divination** (guidance.mdo3d.com) | A curious consumer, for reflection or fun | Warm, plainspoken, never woo or absolute; frames readings as prompts for reflection, never prediction | A grounded observation about the practice | An invitation to reflect, never a claim about the future |
| **Leads** (leads.mdo3d.com) | A B2B seller/marketer | Direct, ROI-aware, unhyped | The specific workflow it speeds | A concrete outcome |

Whichever brand, the mechanical rules in section 2 always apply.

## 2. Mechanical rules (live, enforced by the QC gate)

A draft carrying any of these is flagged before review:

1. **Em and en dashes** in the body. Use separate sentences, commas, or parentheses.
2. **Contrastive reframing.** Any "not X, it is Y" / "not just X" / "not A, but B",
   including the dash form. State directly what a thing is; do not define it by what it
   is not.
3. **Performance claims about our own tool** framed as ours (no "10x faster", no "94%").
   Cite an external study instead.
4. **Adoption or user-count metrics** (no "trusted by", no "N users").
5. **Product/brand hashtags** (#mdo3d, #rigor, ...) and **compound/camelCase hashtags**.
   Use single searchable words or field acronyms, four to five per post.
