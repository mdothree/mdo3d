**Status:** Scaffold. The mechanical rules below are live and enforced by
`review/voice_qc.py`. The brand register and per-tool claims are TODO, to be filled when
MDO3D social content and its brand voice are defined.

**Scope:** Social and outbound copy for the MDO3D portfolio.

## 2. Mechanical rules (live, enforced by the QC gate)

A draft carrying any of these is flagged before review:

1. **Em and en dashes** in the body. Use separate sentences, commas, or parentheses.
2. **Contrastive reframing.** Any "not X, it is Y" / "not just X" / "not A, but B",
   including the dash form. State directly what a thing is; do not define it by what it
   is not.
3. **Performance claims about our own tool** framed as ours (no "10x faster", no "94%").
   Cite an external study instead.
4. **Adoption or user-count metrics** (no "trusted by", no "N users").
5. **Product/brand hashtags** (#mdo3d, #rigor, ...) and **compound/camelCase hashtags**.
   Use single searchable words or field acronyms, four to five per post.
