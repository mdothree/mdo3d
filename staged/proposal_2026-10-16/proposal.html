What a visitor finds today

We fetched 40 MDO hostnames this morning. 38 answer with a page. Most of what follows comes from those pages, read the way a first-time buyer reads them.

Working well

  • Every reading site has a working checkout, and the API checks the payment before it writes the reading.
  • LayoffLeads and Prompts for Business sell at clean prices: $49 / $490 and $14 / $59.
  • Daily AI Toll is the closest to the bar: restrained, monospace, one big number (290,574).
  • The Rigor tools no longer link to a Stripe checkout. Their button reads “Pro: coming soon”.
  • The BlackLabb footer on numerology is gone.

Costing trust

  • Dreams shows $4.99 and a “Basic $0” plan. Names shows $2.99.
  • Tarot charges $10 for three cards and $10 for ten. Runes charges $10 for both three and five.
  • Feng Shui and Past Life show no price until you submit the form.
  • None of the reading sites shows a card, rune or hexagram.
  • api.rigor.design/api/health returns 404, so the Rigor tools fail after sign-in.
  • leads.mdo3d.com returns 530 (the tunnel is down).

Spread thin

  • 33 consumer hostnames serve about 3 real products.
  • We counted 7 visual languages across them.
  • The hub headline is “One ecosystem. Many dimensions of intelligence.” It says what MDO3D is, not what a visitor can do.
  • jarvisbee.com is a GoDaddy parking page with ad tiles.
  • blacklabb.com returns a Bubble 404. It is out of scope (your call is pending).
