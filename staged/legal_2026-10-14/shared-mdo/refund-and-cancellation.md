# Refund Policy

**Effective date:** [CONFIRM: publish date]

This policy covers purchases on mdo3d.com and the sites that link to it: Guidance readings, Baby Name Oracle reports, and Rigor Pro.

## Sales are final, with these exceptions
Because results are digital and delivered right away, sales are final and we do not refund unused time, partly used periods or periods left after you cancel. **We will refund you in full, whenever you ask, if:**
- **it never arrived:** you paid and the reading, report or Pro access was not delivered, and we cannot fix it within 2 business days of you telling us;
- **you were charged in error:** a duplicate charge for the same purchase, or a charge you did not authorise; or
- **the law requires it:** any refund or cancellation right you have that cannot be waived.

**Lost a Guidance unlock?** Your unlock is saved in the browser you paid in. If you cleared it, changed device, or the reading failed to load, email us with your checkout email: we will restore the reading, or refund it if we cannot.

**Baby Name Oracle:** use "find my report" with your checkout email and card's last 4 digits; if that fails, email us.

## How to ask
Email hello@mdo3d.com from the address you paid with (or forward your Stripe receipt). Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually arrive within 5–10 business days. Please email us before disputing a charge with your bank; we usually resolve it faster.

# Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** [CONFIRM: publish date]

**One-time purchases** (Guidance readings, Baby Name Oracle reports) never renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

**Rigor Pro** is our only subscription. [CONFIRM: while Pro checkout shows "coming soon", keep this sentence: "Rigor Pro is not on sale yet; this section applies once it opens."]
- Cancel at any time: in any Rigor tool open the plans panel → **Manage Plan** (Stripe billing portal), or email hello@mdo3d.com from your account address and we will cancel it and confirm by email.
- Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for; the current period is not refunded (see the Refund Policy for exceptions).
- To avoid the next charge, cancel before your renewal date. Renewal reminders: [CONFIRM: whether Stripe renewal-reminder emails are switched on for annual plans].
- After the period ends your account stays and you can sign in, but the AI tools need an active subscription.
- Upgrades may take effect immediately with a prorated charge; downgrades take effect at the next renewal.
