1 · What the reviews found

Seven visual languages, no shared system

Consumer audit (10-14)

  • At least 7 looks across 33 hostnames (hub purple SaaS, guidance editorial, divination gradients, Next.js plain, names unstyled, mdothree mono-green, rigor navy/gold).
  • names.mdo3d.com scored 1/5: browser defaults, no header or brand.
  • rigor.design hub 2/5: a bare grid of 7 “Beta” emoji cards, no nav, no footer.
  • mdo3d.com hub: purple gradient, emoji icons, “31 live platforms”.
  • BlackLabb strings leaked onto MDO reading apps.

B2B audit (10-14)

  • Ronna 2/5: gradient hero, emoji icons, “5 / 1 / Soon” stat row, blank /pricing, Sign In and Upgrade next to “not open yet”.
  • LayoffLeads and Prompts for Business share a generic dark SaaS template (3/5).
  • Daily AI Toll is the closest to the bar: restrained and monospace (4/5).
  • leads.mdo3d.com works (FL, CO, CT filings) and is the natural engine for Ronna.

Before/after proposal (10-16)

  • MDO design system v0: shared header, footer, label style, 3px corners, hairlines.
  • Per-product voice: Guidance (Cormorant/EB Garamond, ink-violet, gold), Data (Plex, paper, red), index (ink, paper, gold).
  • Real imagery: card art, a real WARN chart, real rows.
  • blacklabb.com out of scope: its future is undecided.
2 · The brand as data

Five MDO families and one Blacklab B identity

Shared throughline for MDO: Inter Tight labels and UI, IBM Plex Mono numbers, radius 2–4px, 1px hairlines, 44px tap targets, one offer and one next step. Blacklab B has none of the MDO faces or colours.
